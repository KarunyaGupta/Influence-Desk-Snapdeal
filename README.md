# Influence-Desk-Snapdeal
Led the 0-to-1 development of Snapdeal's Influencer Hub, a centralized platform designed to replace the existing email-driven influencer management process.

• Identified key challenges including communication gaps, missed transaction deadlines, fragmented campaign tracking, and manual coordination across Business and Finance teams.

• Designed the complete influencer lifecycle covering influencer registration, campaign assignment, campaign tracking, transaction management, payment processing, and stakeholder remarks.Collaborated with Business, Finance, and cross-functional stakeholders to translate business requirements into a structured digital workflow.

• Took ownership from problem identification and requirements gathering to product development, testing, deployment, and go-live, with the platform now live for organizational use.
